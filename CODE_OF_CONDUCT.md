# Code of Conduct

The BRAC University NLP Reading Group aims to foster a respectful, inclusive, rigorous academic discussion.

- Treat all participants respectfully, regardless of experience level, background, or institutional affiliation.
- Critique research questions, methods, evidence, assumptions, and reproducibility; do not attack people.
- Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, threats, or unwanted personal attention.
- Respect intellectual property, unpublished work, and requests not to record or redistribute content.
- Give participants room to ask questions and acknowledge uncertainty when evidence is limited.

For concerns, contact the group organizers when they have been announced; until then, avoid posting sensitive reports as public GitHub issues. GitHub's platform reporting mechanisms may be used for violations occurring on GitHub.

Organizers may moderate discussions and contributions to uphold these expectations.
