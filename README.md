# BRAC University NLP Reading Group

**Reading, discussing, and critically evaluating research in Natural Language Processing, Computational Linguistics, and Large Language Models.**

An open, community-organized reading group for BRAC University students, researchers, and faculty, as well as other interested participants. The aim is to understand influential and recent papers, question their assumptions and evidence, and identify opportunities for future research.

> **Group setup:** Meeting day, time, venue, online link, and organizers are **to be announced**. This repository is a starting point, not a record of meetings already held.

**Quick links:** [Reading list](#seed-reading-list--fall-2026) · [Candidate paper library](papers/README.md) · [How to participate](#how-to-participate) · [Presentation guide](docs/PRESENTATION_GUIDE.md) · [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Reference archive](archive/REFERENCE_SCHEDULES.md)

## About the group

We discuss papers spanning language modeling, reasoning, interpretability, representation learning, multilingual NLP, evaluation, alignment, and neighboring research areas. Participants are encouraged to read the assigned paper in advance; all levels of research experience are welcome.

The format is intentionally lightweight:

1. **Before the session:** Read the paper and prepare one or two questions.
2. **Paper walkthrough:** A volunteer introduces the problem, method, experiments, and contributions.
3. **Critical discussion:** Evaluate baselines, assumptions, limitations, reproducibility, and open questions.
4. **After the session:** Share slides or notes through this repository when available.

A suggested session structure is **20–30 minutes of presentation and 20–30 minutes of discussion**, to be adjusted once the group confirms its schedule.

## How to participate

- **Attend and discuss:** Read the scheduled paper beforehand; bring questions and constructive critiques.
- **Present:** Volunteer for a paper by opening an [issue](https://github.com/SyedNaveedMahmood/bracu-nlp-reading-group/issues/new/choose) or contacting the organizers once a communication channel is announced.
- **Suggest papers:** Use the [paper suggestion issue template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/paper_suggestion.md) or submit a pull request.
- **Share notes and slides:** Put your session material under `presentations/` when available, and add a link to the relevant row in the reading list.

See the [presentation guide](docs/PRESENTATION_GUIDE.md) for a reproducible and critical paper-review structure.
