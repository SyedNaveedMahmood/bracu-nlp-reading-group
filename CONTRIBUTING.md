# Contributing to the BRAC University NLP Reading Group

We welcome paper suggestions, factual corrections, critical reading notes, session slides, and improvements to organization.

## Propose a paper
1. Search the [current reading list](README.md#seed-reading-list--fall-2026) and [candidate library](papers/README.md) for duplicates.
2. Open a [paper suggestion issue](https://github.com/SyedNaveedMahmood/bracu-nlp-reading-group/issues/new/choose). Include the paper title, link to an authorized copy, and a short reason to discuss it.
3. Add your name as a prospective presenter **only if you personally volunteer**. Otherwise leave it as `TBA`.

## Update the schedule
Edit the table in `README.md`. Preserve the verified paper title and URL unless correcting an error. Fill the presenter, date, or slides column only when confirmed by organizers. Do not represent the imported CLU Lab schedule as past BRAC sessions.

## Share a presentation
1. Create a directory named `presentations/YYYY-MM-DD-short-title/` once a real date has been confirmed.
2. Add a `README.md` with the paper link, presenter, key claims, experimental setup, critique, and discussion questions. Link slides that you have permission to share.
3. Link the new notes from the corresponding row in `README.md`.

See [docs/PRESENTATION_GUIDE.md](docs/PRESENTATION_GUIDE.md) for the suggested format.

## Pull requests
Use a concise title such as `Add paper: <title>` or `Update session: <date>`. Describe what changed, cite original sources for papers, and check that Markdown links resolve. Avoid adding copyrighted PDFs or third-party presentation decks without permission.

## Community expectations
Disagree with arguments, not people. Recognize uncertainty, avoid overclaiming results, and give credit to authors and presenters. Read [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
